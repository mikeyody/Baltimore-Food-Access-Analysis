## Method

Each component is oriented so that higher = more need, then standardized across Baltimore's scored tracts (zscore, clipped to +/-3). The need score is the weighted sum. It is **relative**: it ranks tracts against each other within Baltimore, not against a national standard.

| Component | Measure (source) |
|---|---|
| food_access | Miles from population-weighted centroid to nearest full-service grocery store (2026 SNAP list) |
| health | Average standardized rate of diabetes, obesity, high blood pressure, coronary heart disease (CDC PLACES 2025) |
| poverty | % of residents below the poverty line (ACS 2020-24) |
| no_car | % of households without a vehicle (ACS 2020-24) |
| seniors | % of residents aged 65+ (ACS 2020-24) |

Default weights (rationale in `src/config.py`): access is the core question (largest weight); diet-related disease is what better access aims to improve; poverty limits what people can buy; no car multiplies the distance problem; the 65+ share is a weaker, indirect mobility signal (smallest weight).
